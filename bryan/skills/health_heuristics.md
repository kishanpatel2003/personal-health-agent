# Interpreting longitudinal records

Compare like-for-like measurements and preserve units, dates, provenance, and
uncertainty. Distinguish reported facts from interpretations. A short change in a
noisy measurement does not establish a causal explanation or diagnosis. Ask for
missing context before recommending a behavioral change. Do not assume any diet,
body-composition goal, target, injury, or medical history. Escalate urgent symptoms
as described in the system prompt rather than generating a confident explanation.
